Background

About Me

Born in Egypt, educated in Toronto — I am a physicist, educator, and entrepreneur passionate about sustainable energy, scientific outreach, and the power of mentorship. Member of CAP, APS, and IEEE. Fluent in English and Arabic, conversational in French and Spanish.

“

If you want to understand something, teach it.

— Richard Feynman

After encountering Feynman's words, I accentuated my passion for teaching — hosting a significant portion of my life with students from grade nine through third-year university, mostly in science and mathematics. My time passes remarkably fast when discussing such ideas, sparking new ways of thinking about the universe. It's a marvellous cycle. I always strive to provide my students with deeper-level understanding and, more saliently, genuine appreciation through our exuberant discussions.
